# Contacting support and response times

## How to contact us

Open a ticket in the app or at support.taskflow.example. Choose the category that fits best: it
sends your ticket to the right team faster.

## Support hours

The support team works Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 UTC. Security tickets are handled 24 hours
a day, 7 days a week.

## First response times

| Priority | First response |
|---|---|
| Critical | within 1 hour |
| High | within 4 hours |
| Medium | within 1 business day |
| Low | within 3 business days |

Business plan customers get responses twice as fast for High and Medium tickets.

## Service status

Check status.taskflow.example to see whether there is an outage before opening a ticket.
