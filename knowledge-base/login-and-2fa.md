# Signing in, passwords and two-factor authentication

## Forgot your password

Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your email. The reset link arrives within a
few minutes and is valid for 1 hour. If no email arrives, check the spam folder and make sure you
typed the email you signed up with.

## Account locked

After 5 wrong passwords in a row, the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait 15 minutes, or reset
your password to unlock it right away.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

2FA adds a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Authy) when you
sign in. Turn it on in Settings > Security.

- **Code rejected?** The most common cause is the phone's clock being wrong. Turn on automatic time
  in the phone's settings.
- **Lost your phone?** Use one of the 10 backup codes you saved when you turned on 2FA. Each backup
  code works once. If you have no backup codes, open a ticket: support will verify your identity
  before turning 2FA off, which takes up to 2 business days.

## Signing in with Google

If "Sign in with Google" brings you back to the sign-in page, allow third-party cookies for
taskflow.example, or sign in with your email and password instead.

## Staying signed in

"Remember me" keeps you signed in for 30 days on that browser. Clearing cookies signs you out.
