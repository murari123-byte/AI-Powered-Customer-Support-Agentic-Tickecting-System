# Billing and invoices

## When you are charged

You are charged on the same day each month (monthly plans) or each year (annual plans), starting
from the day you first subscribed. We send a reminder email 7 days before an annual renewal.

## Invoices

Every payment creates an invoice. Download invoices from Settings > Billing > Invoices, as PDF.
Invoices from the last 7 years are kept.

To show your company name, address or VAT number on invoices, add them in Settings > Billing >
Company details. The change applies to new invoices only; old invoices cannot be edited.

## Changing plans

- **Upgrading** takes effect immediately. You pay the difference for the rest of the current period.
- **Downgrading** takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You keep your current
  plan's features until then.

## Seats

Business plans are billed per active user ("seat"). Removing a user frees the seat from the next
billing period. Deactivated users are not counted.

## Discount codes

Discount codes must be entered at checkout. A code can't be added to a payment that has already
been made. If you forgot to use a code, contact Billing within 7 days of the payment.
