# Payment methods

## What you can pay with

TaskFlow accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit or debit cards, and PayPal.
Annual Business plans can also be paid by bank transfer: ask the Billing Team for an invoice with
our bank details. Apple Pay and Google Pay are not supported yet.

## When a card is declined

Most declines come from the bank, not from us. Please check:

1. The card has not expired, and the billing address matches the one your bank has.
2. Your bank allows online payments in US dollars (all prices are in USD).
3. The 3D Secure check from your bank was completed. If its window stays blank, turn off
   pop-up blockers and try again, or use another browser.

If the payment still fails, try another card or PayPal, or open a ticket with the exact error message.

## Failed renewal payments

If a renewal payment fails, we try again after 1, 3 and 7 days. Your account keeps working during
this time. After the last failed try, the account moves to the Free plan; no data is deleted, and
paying again restores the plan.

## Money taken but payment shows as failed

Sometimes the bank reserves the money even though the payment failed. This reservation is released
by the bank within 7 days. If the money was really taken, open a Billing ticket and we will refund it.
