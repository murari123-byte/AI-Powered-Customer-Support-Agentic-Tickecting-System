# Refund policy

TaskFlow wants you to be happy with your plan. This page explains when you can get your money back.

## Monthly plans

Monthly plans can be refunded within 14 days of the payment date. After 14 days, the payment is not
refunded, but you can cancel so you are not charged again. Your plan keeps working until the end of
the month you paid for.

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, we refund the unused
full months, minus a 10% administration fee. Example: if you cancel after 4 months, you get back 8
months of the price, minus 10%.

## Double charges

If you were charged twice for the same period, we always refund the extra charge, no matter how
long ago it happened. Send us the date and amount of both charges.

## How long a refund takes

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with. They show up in 5 to 10 business days,
depending on your bank. We can't speed this up, but we can send you a refund receipt.

## How to ask for a refund

Open a ticket with the category Billing, and include your invoice number. Refunds can only be
approved by the Billing Team.
