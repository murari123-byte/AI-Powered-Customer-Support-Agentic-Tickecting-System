# Managing your account and workspace

## Change your email address

Go to Settings > Profile > Email, type the new address, and confirm it by clicking the link we send
to it. Until you confirm, the old address stays active.

## Invite and remove team members

Workspace admins can invite people from Settings > Members > Invite. The invitation link is valid
for 7 days. To remove someone, open Settings > Members, choose the person, and click Remove. They
lose access immediately, and their tasks stay in the workspace.

## Transfer ownership

Only the current owner can transfer ownership: Settings > Workspace > Transfer ownership. If the
owner has left the company, a workspace admin can open a ticket; support will ask for proof from
your company (for example an email from your company domain) before transferring it.

## Delete your account

Settings > Profile > Delete account. Deletion is permanent after 30 days; during those 30 days you
can sign in again to cancel it. We delete all personal data within 30 days, as required by GDPR.

## Time zone

Your time zone is set in Settings > Profile > Time zone. Reports and due dates use it.
