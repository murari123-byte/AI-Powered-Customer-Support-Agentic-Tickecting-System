# Features: frequently asked questions

## Can I import data from Excel or CSV?

Yes. Go to Projects > Import and upload a .xlsx or .csv file with up to 10,000 rows. The first row
must contain the column names.

## Is there a limit on projects?

The Free plan allows 3 projects. Starter allows 20 projects. Pro and Business allow unlimited projects.

## Recurring reports

On Pro and Business plans, open any report and click Schedule to have it emailed daily, weekly or
monthly.

## Integrations

TaskFlow integrates with Slack, Google Drive, GitHub and Zapier. Connect them in Settings >
Integrations. A calendar view and Google Sheets export are on the roadmap but not available yet.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press "?" anywhere in the app to see all keyboard shortcuts.

## Exporting

Reports can be exported as PDF or CSV. Files larger than 50 MB are emailed to you instead of downloaded.
